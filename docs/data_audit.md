## Orderbook Observations

**1. Tick-size evidence**

Adjacent displayed price levels are overwhelmingly separated by **0.10
quote-currency units**.

| Date | 0.10 | 0.20 | 0.30 | 0.40 | 0.50 |
|---|---:|---:|---:|---:|---:|
| 2026-03-19 | 92.29% | 6.86% | 0.73% | 0.10% | 0.02% |
| 2026-03-20 | 92.91% | 6.34% | 0.62% | 0.09% | 0.02% |
| 2026-03-21 | 91.52% | 6.86% | 1.31% | 0.23% | 0.06% |

The observed price increments imply an inferred tick size of **0.10
quote-currency units**. Larger gaps occur because intermediate price levels
contain no displayed liquidity, not because the exchange uses a larger tick
size.

**2. Spread evidence**

The top-of-book spread distribution provides consistent supporting evidence:

| Date | Median spread | 99th percentile | Maximum spread |
|---|---:|---:|---:|
| 2026-03-19 | 0.10 | 0.10 | 2.70 |
| 2026-03-20 | 0.10 | 0.10 | 1.90 |
| 2026-03-21 | 0.10 | 0.10 | 2.60 |

All spread values are in quote-currency units. The median and 99th-percentile
spread are **0.10** on every day. The larger maxima show that the market
occasionally becomes substantially wider, but these observations lie in the
extreme upper tail.

**3. Update frequency**

Order book update intervals vary by day:

| Date | Median update interval | Longest observed gap |
|---|---:|---:|
| 2026-03-19 | 52 ms | 22.65 s |
| 2026-03-20 | 53 ms | 36.91 s |
